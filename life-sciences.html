FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask.

What is commercial intelligence in life sciences?
It is the layer that connects market data, field activity and scientific evidence to the commercial decisions a pharma or biopharma team needs to make. Tecera combines governed data, domain analytics and continuous delivery rather than treating each source as a separate report.
What does a Forward-Deployed Consultant do in pharma?
They work directly with the commercial team, understand the business question, build analytics or applications inside the client's existing environment, and remain involved as the intelligence evolves. The role combines pharma commercial context, hands-on analytics and technical delivery.
Can Tecera work with our existing Veeva, Power BI, Snowflake or Databricks environment?
Yes. The model is intentionally stack-agnostic. Tecera can work from flat files and existing BI through to modern cloud and agentic environments without forcing a platform migration.
How is Tecera different from a typical analytics consultancy or software platform?
Tecera operates as a continuous intelligence function. We do not require a new platform licence and we do not treat delivery as a one-off analytics project. The same team can diagnose, build, deploy and evolve the intelligence with the client.
Where does AI fit into the Tecera model?
AI is applied only where the commercial decision benefits from it, for example forecasting, NLP, literature monitoring or pattern detection at scale. The foundation and domain logic come first.
